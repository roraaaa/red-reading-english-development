RED — Reading English Development

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

RED is an English reading-comprehension app. To save your scores it needs an account, so it stores your name, your email address and the results of the stories and assessments you finish. That is all. RED shows no adverts, has no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your information with anyone who wants to market to you. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who runs RED

RED is built and run by Clarence Purisima as an independent project. If you have a question about this policy or about your information, write to clarencepurisimacp@gmail.com.

What RED collects

Information Why
Your name (or whatever you type as your name) To greet you and label your progress
Your email address To log you in and to send a password-reset link if you ask for one
Your password Handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it in a scrambled (hashed) form. RED never sees or stores your actual password.
Your results: which story or assessment you did, your score, stars, the reading time and reading speed, and when you finished To show your progress over time on the My progress screen

What RED does not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers, and no adverts of any kind
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No payment information — RED is free and has nothing to buy

Where your information is kept

RED uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store accounts and scores. Google processes this information on RED's behalf and on its own servers, which may be outside your country. Google describes how it handles this data in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

Your device also keeps a copy of the stories and your login session so the app works offline. Uninstalling RED removes that local copy, but it does not delete your account — see below.

Other services the app touches

  • Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech voice already installed on your device. The text is spoken on the device; nothing is uploaded, and RED does not record any audio.
  • Google Fonts supplies the two typefaces RED uses. Your device downloads them from Google's servers the first time they are needed, which means Google receives that request in the ordinary way any website request works.
  • Printing and PDFs are produced on your device and handed to whatever printing or file app you choose. RED does not upload them.
  • Story photographs are included inside the app itself under open licences, with the photographer and licence credited on each story. No request leaves your device to show them.

Deleting your account and your scores

You can delete everything yourself, at any time, without asking anyone:

  • Open RED and log in.
  • Tap the circle with your initial at the top of the home screen.
  • Tap Delete my account, then confirm with your password.

This permanently removes your account, your name, your email address and every score you have saved. It cannot be undone, and there is no backup copy to restore from.

If you can no longer open the app — for example you have uninstalled it or cannot log in — email clarencepurisimacp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking for your account to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

How long information is kept

Your account and scores are kept for as long as the account exists, because the whole point is to show progress over months of reading. When you delete the account, the account record and its scores are removed from the live database immediately. Google's own backups of Firestore may keep a copy for a short period afterwards before they expire.

Age

RED is intended for readers aged 13 and over, and for teachers, tutors and parents who use it with a learner. It is not directed at children under 13, and RED does not knowingly collect information from a child under 13. If you believe a younger child has created an account, email us at the address above and we will delete it.

A note for teachers and parents: if you want a younger learner to use RED, please create and hold the account yourself and supervise its use. That way the account is yours, and you decide what name and email address it carries.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, data-protection law gives you rights over your information. Wherever you are, RED offers all of the following:

  • See it — your name and email are on the Account screen and every score is on My progress.
  • Correct it — email us and we will fix anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it — use Delete my account, or email us.
  • Take it with you — every story and result can be saved as a PDF from inside the app, and we will send you a copy of your stored data on request.
  • Complain — you can raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

RED is operated from the Philippines and follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Readers in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have these rights under the GDPR; the lawful basis for handling your information is the contract to provide you the app, and your consent to create an account.

Security

Traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted. Database rules mean an account can only ever read and write its own profile and its own scores, and a saved score cannot be edited after the fact. No system is perfect, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what RED collects or who it shares with will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
